✨ What Is AirCard?

AirCard is a simple, powerful Windows app that lets you change how your iPhone looks and feels. With AirCard, you can:

  • 🎨 Custom Card Skins – Put your own artwork, textures, or favorite bank logos on your Apple Pay and Apple Cash cards. Make your wallet stand out.
  • 🔢 Lock Screen Passcode Themes – Apply cool keypad button designs (from popular Cowabunga & Nugget themes) to your iPhone's lock screen. Change the look of the numbers you tap every day.
  • ⚡ 100% Native & Lightweight – AirCard is a single, tiny file (about 7.5 MB). No complicated setups, no extra software, no bloat. It just works.
  • 🪟 Beautiful & Easy to Use – The app has a clean, modern dark design that's simple to navigate. If you can use a web browser, you can use AirCard.
  • 📱 Works with Your iPhone Instantly – Just tap any card in your iPhone's Wallet app while connected to your PC, and AirCard will detect it automatically. No technical know-how required.
  • 📶 USB or WiFi Connection – Connect your iPhone with a cable or over your home WiFi. AirCard handles both.
  • 🌐 English & Simplified Chinese – Use the app in the language you're most comfortable with.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this safe? Will it break my iPhone?

AirCard is completely safe. It uses a well-known, widely tested method to change visual elements on your iPhone. It does not modify any critical system files, and your phone will continue to work normally. If you ever want to go back to the default look, simply restart your iPhone or re-apply the original theme.

Do I need to jailbreak my iPhone?

No! AirCard works without jailbreaking. That's one of its best features. It uses a clever (but completely legal) synchronization method to apply changes.

What if I don't have an Apple Pay card?

No problem. You can still use AirCard to change your lock screen passcode theme. That feature works independently of your wallet cards.

Can I use my own images for card skins?

Absolutely! AirCard lets you upload any image you like. Use your favorite photo, a logo, or a cool texture – the possibilities are endless.

🛠️ Troubleshooting Tips

If you run into any hiccups, try these simple fixes:

  • iPhone not detected? Make sure you've unlocked your iPhone and tapped "Trust This Computer" when you plug it in. Also check that your USB cable is working properly.
  • App won't start? Right-click the aircard.exe file and select "Run as administrator". This sometimes helps with permission issues.
  • Changes not showing? Try restarting your iPhone. If that doesn't work, disconnect and reconnect your device, then try again.
